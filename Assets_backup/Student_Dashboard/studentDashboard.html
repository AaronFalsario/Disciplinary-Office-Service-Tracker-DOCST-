First Offense Warning Information

What does "Warning Only" mean?
A first offense is considered a warning. No community service hours are required. However, this serves as an official notice to remind you to follow the student code of conduct.
What happens next?
This warning will remain in your record. Future violations may result in increased penalties, including community service hours or disciplinary actions.
How to avoid further penalties?
Familiarize yourself with the student handbook. Follow school policies and maintain good conduct. If you have questions, consult the Student Affairs Office.
